# Contributing to TypeLang / WriterLang

Thank you for your interest in contributing! Before you submit a pull request, please read the following:

- By contributing, you agree to the terms of the [Contributor License Agreement (CLA)](./CLA.md).
- Contributions are **voluntary and unpaid** unless otherwise stated by external sponsors.
- You **cannot sue** the maintainers for any bugs or issues caused by your own contributions.
- Any legal action against the project maintainers is **against our policy** and will be considered a violation of this agreement.

---

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo.
2. Make your changes and test them.
3. Open a Pull Request (PR) with a description of your changes.

We appreciate your contributions to this project!
